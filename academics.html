Admission Requirements

General criteria for undergraduate and postgraduate programs

Undergraduate Programs

  • ✓ Senior Secondary School Certificate (SSSC) or equivalent
  • ✓ Minimum of 5 credits in relevant subjects
  • ✓ Excellent performance in entrance exams
  • ✓ Statement of purpose
  • ✓ Age 17 and above

Postgraduate Programs

  • ✓ Bachelor's degree from a recognized institution
  • ✓ Minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0
  • ✓ Letters of recommendation
  • ✓ Statement of purpose and research interests
  • ✓ Relevant professional experience (varies by program)
